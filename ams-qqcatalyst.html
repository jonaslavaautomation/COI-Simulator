Getting Started - AMS COI Trainer

This simulates your agency's real QQCatalyst® portal. A client emailed in asking for a Certificate of Insurance - process it exactly like you would on the job.

  • Search for the customer in the Search widget on your Dashboard.
  • Open their Contact record. The client's request is logged under the Emails tab; the policy details you're allowed to certify are under the Policies tab.
  • Click Add ACORD Form, pick 25 - CERTIFICATE OF LIABILITY INSURANCE, then associate only the policies that actually exist for this account.
  • Complete the certificate. The AMS auto-fills your agency and account information for you - the coverage details are your job.
  • Only enter what the policy file actually supports, even if the client's email asks for more.
